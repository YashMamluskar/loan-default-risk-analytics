# Portfolio Summary

## LinkedIn

Built an end-to-end credit risk analytics project on 2.26 million LendingClub loans ($34B funded): a Python and DuckDB pipeline, a full-population MySQL database with 35 business queries, and an interactive Power BI dashboard, with KPIs reconciled across all three tools.

The most useful finding was about measurement. The resolved-loan default rate is 20.0%, but it falls to 15.2% on loans whose full term has elapsed, because recent resolved loans over-represent early failures. The High risk tier holds 39% of funding but 66% of charged-off principal.
